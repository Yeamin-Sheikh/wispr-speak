Voice Commands and Shortcuts

Voice Commands: Say these commands during or at the end of speech:

  • "new line" inserts a single line break.
  • "new paragraph" inserts a double line break.
  • "scratch that" or "undo that" sends Ctrl+Z to revert the last text injection.
  • "delete word" deletes the word immediately behind the cursor.
  • Spoken punctuation: say "comma", "period", "question mark", "exclamation mark", "colon", or "semicolon".

Selection Polishing: Highlight text in any application (browser, IDE, document, or chat) and press Win + Alt + Q to instantly fix grammar, flow, spelling, and punctuation.

Hands-Free Mode: Tap Ctrl + Win + Space once to begin dictating without holding any keys. When done, tap Ctrl + Win + Space again to transcribe and inject directly at your cursor.
