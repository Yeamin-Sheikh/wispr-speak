Voice Commands & Help

Voice Commands: Say these words during or at the end of speech:

  • "new line" — inserts a single line break
  • "new paragraph" — inserts a double line break
  • "scratch that" — reverts the last injection using Ctrl+Z
  • "undo that" — sends Ctrl+Z to the target application
  • "delete word" — deletes the word behind the cursor (Ctrl+Backspace)
  • Punctuation — say "comma", "period", "question mark", "exclamation mark", "colon", or "semicolon"
